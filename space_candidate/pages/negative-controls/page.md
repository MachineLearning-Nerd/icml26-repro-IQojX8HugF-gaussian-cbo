## Negative controls and limitations

The gate fails if any notebook has an error, lacks Ntests=100, omits a baseline trajectory, has malformed/non-finite raw arrays, or does not show CBO < BW final median KL. C3 is explicitly non-passing because of the contract/source theorem-number mismatch. No finite run is presented as a proof of a universal theorem.
