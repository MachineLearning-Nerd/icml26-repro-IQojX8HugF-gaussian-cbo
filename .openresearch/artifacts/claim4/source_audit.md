# Claim 4 source audit

Primary-source hashes and retrieval details are identical to Claim 3. The
official PDF calls `l:well` **Lemma 3.1**. It literally assumes
`E|m_0^i|^2, E||T_0^i||^2_{Sigma0} < 0`, then requires the displayed
growth-weighted LOT-Lipschitz inequality for every pair of `P2` measures.

Both squared quantities are pointwise nonnegative, so their expectations are
nonnegative. No probability distribution can satisfy the printed strict
negative premise. The likely intended condition is finite moments (`< infinity`),
but silently repairing it would test a different claim.

The ar5iv v2 HTML renders this as Lemma 4.1, conflicting with the official PDF
and TeX structure. The vacuous printed premise is independent of numbering.
